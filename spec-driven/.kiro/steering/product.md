# Lumen Ideas: Product

Source: client brief from Aina Rahman, Head of Innovation, Velox Group (2 Dec 2026). Aina has the final say on scope. Business needs only; how to build it is the delivery team's call.

## What it is
A single place where Velox Group staff send ideas for working better, see what colleagues have sent, and back the ones they like. Velox is a Malaysian logistics company with about 2,000 staff. Today ideas arrive by email, WhatsApp and corridor chats.

## Who uses it
- **Staff**: send an idea in a minute, browse others' ideas, back the ones they like.
- **Innovation committee**: once a month, sees the most-backed ideas to decide where to start.
- **App team** (designer: Daniel Wong): designs the portal screens and needs a service behind them.

## Problems it solves
- Ideas get lost and nobody can see what was already suggested.
- The same idea arrives repeatedly (e.g. "Reusable parcel bags" came in five times last quarter).
- Nobody knows why an idea was picked, and staff want to see what colleagues back.

## Release 1 scope
Brief says: send, browse, then backing ideas. Anything else waits.
- **Send an idea** (first): short title, short description, one category from the fixed list.
- **Browse ideas** (first): every idea, newest first, with sender and their department.
- **Open one idea** (first): all details of a single idea.
- **Back an idea** (next): a staff member backs an idea; each idea shows its backer count.
- **See the favourites** (next): ranked list, most backed first.

## Out of scope for release 1
- Comments, editing or deleting ideas, attachments.
- Anonymous ideas (People and Culture is reviewing the policy).
- AI summaries or any other AI feature.
- Prizes, points or rankings of people.
- Staff sign-in. For now the staff ID is trusted.

## Business rules
- Exactly five categories: Cost saving, Customer experience, Safety, Process, Sustainability. The list is fixed because the committee reports by category monthly.
- Sender name and department come from the HR staff list, never from what someone types.

## Success looks like
- Staff use this instead of email and WhatsApp.
- The same idea is not sent in five times.
- Each month the committee can see which ideas staff back most, and why one was picked.

## Open questions (raise with the client before building)
- Maximum length of an idea title, and what happens when it is too long.
- What counts as "the same idea".
- Whether anyone may send an idea without their name.
- Voting rules: who may back what, and how often.
- Brief is unclear whether "back" and "favourites" (marked "Next") are in release 1 or a following step. Confirm with Aina.
- "Why one was picked" is a success measure, but no way of recording a reason is described. Confirm what the committee expects.
