# 8. Retro and next steps

Write it at the bottom of `team-log.md`, then share one item from each:

- **3** things AI-DLC did well for your team

## Habits to take home

1. **Verify the plan, verify the outcome.** Those two human steps are the whole method.
2. **Trim before you approve.** AI over-builds by default. Delete what nobody asked for.
3. **Answer in files.** If it isn't in the audit trail, it didn't happen.
4. **Record the why**, with the source.
5. **Fewer, bigger tasks.** Every task and every agent re-sends its context.
6. **Keep steering short:** `product.md`, `tech.md`, `structure.md`, under about 150 lines together.
7. **Keep your `aidlc-docs/`.** It's the record of why your code does what it does.
